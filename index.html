Why Purging RAM Boosts PC Performance?

Windows often keeps closed apps and background caches stored in 'Standby Memory'. When you launch heavy games or software, Windows has to scramble to clear that memory, causing micro-stutters and FPS drops. TurboCleaner forces an immediate cleanup, giving your game 100% clean RAM immediately.
